0.2task_struct: The Hub Everything Hangs Off

In Linux, every thread of every process is one struct task_struct. Almost every topic is "a field of task_struct and what it points to". Click a field.

struct task_struct
Connection: current is the task_struct running on this CPU; a syscall like read(3, …) finds "your fd 3" through current->files.
