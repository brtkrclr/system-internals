Spinlock or mutex? It depends on how long the lock is held

Rough model: a waiter that sleeps pays about two context switches (~2 µs of CPU, plus ~5 µs wake-up latency; 2.4 latency table). A spinner burns CPU for the whole hold time but resumes instantly. Multi-core only.

Read-write lock: many readers or one writer

Simulation: a new reader arrives every tick and reads for 3 ticks; one writer arrives at tick 2 and writes for 3 ticks.

Connection: the stack is atomic instruction (cache coherence, 2.4) → futex (syscall only on contention, 3.3) → mutex / condvar / semaphore. The kernel's own versions are spinlocks, seqlocks and RCU.
